How to Resize Photo and Signature for Exam Forms (20 KB, 50 KB)

6 min read · Updated October 2026

You've filled the whole application form, and then the portal says: "Signature must be between 10 KB and 20 KB." Your phone photo is 3 MB. Now what?

This guide shows you how to get your photo and signature to the exact size an exam form asks for, for free, in about two minutes, without installing any app.

Fastest way: our photo and signature resizer has ready presets for SSC, IBPS, UPSC, NEET, JEE and RRB, with exact pixels and KB ranges.

Quick answer

  1. Open the free LearnAIFast converter.
  2. Add your photo or signature.
  3. Pick JPG and set "Make each image smaller than" to the limit your form asks for (for example 20 KB for a signature or 50 KB for a photo).
  4. Click Convert, then Download.

Your files are processed inside your browser and never uploaded anywhere, which matters when you're handling ID documents.

Typical size limits

Every exam sets its own rules, and they change between years. These are the limits you'll see most often:

Always check the official notification for your exam before uploading. It has the final word on size, dimensions and format.

Two 2026 examples

  • SSC (for example CGL 2026): the photo is now captured live through a webcam or the mobile app, so you don't upload a photo file at all. The signature is still an upload: JPG, 10–20 KB, about 4 cm × 2 cm, signed in black or blue ink on white paper.
  • IBPS PO 2026: the handwritten declaration must be a JPG of 50–100 KB, written in English in black ink on white paper. PDF is not accepted.

Step by step: resize your signature to 20 KB

  1. Sign on plain white paper with a black or blue pen. Make it bold enough to read; a faint or tiny signature can get your form rejected.
  2. Take a photo in good light. Hold the phone straight above the paper so the signature isn't slanted. Avoid shadows from your hand or phone.
  3. Crop tightly around the signature using your phone's photo editor, leaving only a little white space. A signature is usually wider than it is tall, roughly 2:1.
  4. Open the Signature under 20 KB preset. It sets JPG and the 20 KB limit for you.
  5. Add the photo, click Convert and download. The tool lowers quality and size just enough to fit under the limit.

If your form needs a minimum size too (for example "10–20 KB"), check the downloaded file's size. If it's below the minimum, set the limit one step higher or pick a larger width and try again.

Step by step: resize your photo to 50 KB

  1. Use a recent photo with a plain light background, your face clearly visible, no cap or dark glasses (unless your exam's rules say otherwise).
  2. Crop to passport shape: head and top of shoulders, a little taller than it is wide.
  3. Open the Photo under 50 KB preset.
  4. If your form gives a width in pixels (for example 200 px), choose the nearest option under "Resize to width".
  5. Convert and download.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reduce photo size to 20 KB without losing quality?
Crop away extra background first, then resize to the width your form asks for, then compress. A smaller picture needs less compression, so it stays sharper at 20 KB.

Do I need to install an app to resize my photo?
No. The LearnAIFast converter works in any phone or computer browser, and your photo never leaves your device.

My iPhone photo won't open. What do I do?
iPhones save photos as HEIC by default. Go to Settings → Camera → Formats → "Most Compatible" to save new photos as JPG, or share the photo as JPG first.

What is the size of a passport photo in pixels?
It depends on the exam. Many forms ask for around 200 × 230 pixels, but some ask in centimetres instead. Use the value in your exam's notification.

Conclusion

Crop first, resize second, compress last, and always match the exact limits in your exam's notification. Bookmark the converter so it's ready the next time a form asks for "under 20 KB".
