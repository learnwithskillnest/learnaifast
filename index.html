Explain inflation to a Class 11 student using vegetable prices in India. Under 60 words.
ai

Last year 1 kg of tomatoes cost ₹40. This year it costs ₹50, but your pocket money is the same. That ₹10 jump across most things you buy is inflation.

Quick check: if prices rise 5% a year, what does ₹100 of groceries cost next year?
