About LearnAIFast

AI is changing how we study, work and run businesses. Most AI content online is either too technical or too full of hype. LearnAIFast exists to fix that.

We explain AI in simple language and show you exactly how to use it, step by step. Whether you're a student preparing for exams, a professional who wants to save time at work, or a shop owner trying to grow online, you'll find practical guides you can use the same day.

Our mission

To help one million Indians learn practical AI skills, for free, in the simplest way possible.

What we publish

  • Beginner guides to AI and popular AI tools
  • Step-by-step tutorials with real examples
  • Ready-to-use prompts for study, work and business
  • Honest reviews and comparisons of AI tools
  • A weekly roundup of the most useful AI updates

Who's behind LearnAIFast

LearnAIFast is run by Skillnest. It was founded by a software professional with:

  • M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning from BITS Pilani
