Tips for a clean scan

  • Put the page on a dark surface, like a table or bedsheet, so the edges are easy to find.
  • Use daylight or a bright room and avoid your own shadow on the page.
  • Fill most of the photo with the page. A slight angle is fine; the scanner straightens it.
  • Choose Black & white for handwritten notes and printed text, and Clean scan for certificates with colour stamps or photos.
  • For forms that need a small file, set File size before saving. To compress an existing PDF, use Compress PDF.
