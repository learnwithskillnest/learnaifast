Note: Attempt all Sections. If you require any missing data, then choose suitably.

Section A

Q1. Attempt all questions in brief · 2 × 10 = 20 marks

  1. Define Machine learning and its application.
  2. What do you mean by big data analysis
  3. Define Binary Decision Tree
  4. Discuss the weight and bias in ANN
  5. How do you evaluate hypothesis?
  6. What is Bayes Theorem?
  7. What is finite and infinite hypothesis space?
  8. What is PAC learning model in machine learning?
  9. What is the meaning of Chromosome in genetic algorithm?
  10. What is the difference between reinforcement learning and Artificial Intelligence?

Section B

Q2. Attempt any three of the following · 10 × 3 = 30 marks

  1. What are the five limitations of machine learning? Explain with suitable example.
  2. Define the following in decision tree algorithm: (i) Entropy, (ii) Information gain, (iii) Gini index, (iv) Gain Ratio, (iv) Chi-Square
  3. What is Naive Bayes classifier and how does it work? Explain the advantages of Naive Bayes algorithm.
  4. What is the advantages & disadvantages of locally weighted regression? Explain with suitable example.
  5. Explain the main steps of genetic algorithm with suitable examples?

Section C

Q3

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Explain the difference between Find-S and candidate elimination algorithm?
  2. What is the inductive bias in CNN? Explain the image identification with CNN.
