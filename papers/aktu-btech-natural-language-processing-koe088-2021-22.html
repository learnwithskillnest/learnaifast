AKTU Natural Language Processing (KOE088) Question Paper 2021-22

UniversityDr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow
CourseB.Tech, Semester VIII
SubjectNatural Language Processing (KOE088)
ExamTheory Examination 2021-22 (even semester)
Time · Marks3 hours · 100 marks
SourceAKTU question paper, from the ABESIT question paper bank
View original PDF Other years

Note: Attempt all Sections. If you require any missing data, then choose suitably.

Section A

Q1. Attempt all questions in brief · 10 × 2 = 20 marks

  1. What do you mean by Semantics in Natural Language Processing?
  2. Define the term Natural Language.
  3. Discuss about the concept of knowledge.
  4. What is the concept of Parsing?
  5. What are the Applications of Natural Language Processing (NLP)?
  6. Briefly define Probabilistic Language Processing (PLP).
  7. What do you mean by Grammars for Natural Language?
  8. What is the concept of Top-Down and Bottom-Up Parsers?
  9. What is the need of Verb Phrases in Natural Language Processing?
  10. What do you mean by the Ambiguity?

Section B

Q2. Attempt any three of the following · 10 × 3 = 30 marks

  1. Discuss about the Statistical Methods under the Ambiguity Resolution.
  2. Describe in detail about the Machine Translation.
  3. Describe Feature Systems and Augmented Grammar under Grammars and Parsing.
  4. Describe in detail about the Movement Phenomenon in Language.
  5. What is the Concept of Evaluating Language Understanding Systems?

Section C

Q3

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Discuss in brief about the concept of Probabilistic Context-Free Grammars.
  2. Discuss about the following: (i) Basic Feature system for English. (ii) Morphological Analysis.

Q4

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Discuss in detail about the Different levels of Language Analysis.
  2. Discuss about the following: (i) Transition Network Grammars. (ii) Top- Down Chart Parsing.

Q5

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Describe in detail about Handling questions in Context-Free Grammars.
  2. Discuss in brief about the Word senses and Ambiguity under the Ambiguity Resolution.

Q6

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Discuss about the database interface under the Natural Language Understanding.
  2. Mention about the Organization of Natural language Understanding Systems.

Q7

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Describe about the Augmented Transition Networks.
  2. Describe in detail about the Human preferences in Parsing.
