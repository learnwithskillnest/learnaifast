Answer hints

Q2. What is overfitting?

Overfitting is when a model learns the training data too closely, including its noise, so it scores well on training data but poorly on new data. Reduce it with more training data, cross-validation, regularisation (L1/L2), pruning decision trees, dropout in neural networks, or a simpler model.

Q5. Define entropy and information gain.

Entropy measures how mixed the classes are in a set: E(S) = −Σ pᵢ log₂ pᵢ. It is 0 when all examples share one class and highest when classes are evenly mixed. Information gain is the drop in entropy after splitting on an attribute: Gain(S, A) = E(S) − Σ (|Sᵥ| / |S|) · E(Sᵥ). ID3 splits on the attribute with the highest gain.

Q9. K-means limitations

You must choose K in advance, results depend on the starting centroids, it assumes roughly round clusters of similar size, and outliers pull centroids off. Use the elbow method to pick K and k-means++ for better starting points.
