AKTU Optimization in Machine Learning (KAI071) Question Paper 2023-24

UniversityDr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow
CourseB.Tech, Semester VII
SubjectOptimization in Machine Learning (KAI071)
ExamTheory Examination 2023-24 (odd semester)
Time · Marks3 hours · 100 marks
SourceAKTU question paper, from the ABESIT question paper bank
View original PDF Other years

Note: Attempt all Sections. If require any missing data; then choose suitably.

Section A

Q1. Attempt all questions in brief · 2 × 10 = 20 marks

  1. How does convexity play a crucial role in optimization problems?
  2. Illustrate the application of convex optimization in real-world scenarios.
  3. Explain Nesterov's application in convex optimization.
  4. Investigate Moreau–Yosida regularization.
  5. Explain the regularization process.
  6. Explain the concept of dual decomposition in optimization
  7. How Douglas–Rachford splitting addresses challenges?
  8. How do optimization algorithms strategically navigate saddle points?
  9. What are the implications for convergence and optimization efficiency?
  10. How does the structure of the optimization landscape impact the choice of optimization algorithms?

Section B

Q2. Attempt any three of the following · 10 × 3 = 30 marks

  1. Compare and contrast linear programming, second-order cone programming, 10 and semidefinite programming. Provide real-world examples where each type of convex program is applicable.
  2. Explain the concept of duality in convex optimization. How does duality provide 10 insights into the primal and dual aspects of an optimization problem? Discuss the relationship between the primal and dual solutions.
  3. Compare and contrast mirror descent and traditional gradient descent methods. Highlight the advantages and disadvantages of mirror descent, and provide a real-world example where mirror descent might outperform other optimization algorithms.
  4. Compare the convergence properties of Augmented Lagrangian methods and 10 ADMM. Discuss scenarios where one method might be preferred over the other based on the nature of the optimization problem.
  5. Compare and contrast Polyak–Juditsky averaging with other methods used in 10 stochastic gradient descent. How does it contribute to the convergence and stability of optimization algorithms, especially in the context of deep learning?

Section C

Q3

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Describe the Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) conditions in the context of convex 10 optimization. How do these conditions characterize optimality in convex programs? Provide examples to illustrate the application of KKT conditions.
  2. Provide detailed explanations and examples of different convex programs. Highlight the specific mathematical formulations and problem structures for each type of convex program.

Q4

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Elaborate on the Frank–Wolfe method and its applications in constrained 10 optimization. Discuss how the method addresses challenges posed by large- scale optimization problems and provide a scenario where it is particularly effective.
  2. Explore the concept of Ordinary Differential Equations interpretations in the 10 context of optimization algorithms.

Q5

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Discuss the role of dual methods in optimization and their applications in 10 solving primal-dual problems. Provide an example illustrating the use of dual methods and explain their advantages over primal methods in certain scenarios.
  2. Provide an in-depth overview of proximal gradient methods and their role in 10 handling non-smooth and non-convex optimization problems.

Q6

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Compare and contrast the Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers with 10 other optimization algorithms, highlighting its strengths and weaknesses.
  2. Describe the Douglas–Rachford splitting algorithm and its application in 10 solving convex optimization problems. Discuss situations where this algorithm is particularly effective and the conditions under which it converges.

Q7

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Elaborate on the application of Langevin dynamics in Bayesian inference. How 10 does it relate to escaping saddle points and contribute to efficient sampling in high-dimensional spaces, particularly in the context of deep learning models?
  2. Examine the Bayesian interpretation of Langevin dynamics. How does 10 Langevin dynamics connect to the posterior distribution in Bayesian models, and what are the implications for exploring the posterior space in the context of probabilistic modeling?
