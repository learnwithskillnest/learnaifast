Section A

Q1. Explain the following · 10 × 2 = 20 marks

  1. What is Mean Square Error Restoration?
  2. What do you mean by Dilation?
  3. What is Mathematical Morphology?
  4. What are Opening operations?
  5. What are Closing operations?
  6. What do you understand by Thinning?
  7. What do you understand by Thickening?
  8. What do you mean by Erosion?
  9. Explain about various Degradation functions.
  10. What is meant by Structuring elements?

Section B

Q2. Attempt any five of the following questions · 5 × 10 = 50 marks

  1. What do you understand by Band-Pass Filter?
  2. Draw and Explain Degradation model in detail.

  5. Explain Noise Model in detail.

Section C

Attempt any two of the following questions · 2 × 15 = 30 marks

  1. What do you understand by Hit-Miss Transform and why they are used explain in brief?
