AKTU MCA Data Warehousing & Data Mining (KCA012) Question Paper 2024-25

UniversityDr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow
CourseMCA, Semester III
SubjectData Warehousing & Data Mining (KCA012)
ExamTheory Examination 2024-25 (odd semester)
Time · Marks3 hours · 100 marks
SourceAKTU question paper, from the ABESIT question paper bank
View original PDF Other years

Note: Attempt all Sections. In case of any missing data; choose suitably.

Section A

Q1. Attempt all questions in brief · 2 × 10 = 20 marks

  1. Though we can work with database, why Data Warehouse is required?
  2. What do you mean by data warehouse schema?
  3. Explain Data Cube.
  4. How data mining is different from data warehousing.
  5. Discuss difference between OLTP and OLAP.
  6. Give the reason for noisy data?
  7. Difference between clustering and classification.
  8. Discuss basic assumption in Naïve Bayes Classifiers.
  9. What do you mean by data visualization?
  10. Explain Spatial Data with example.

Section B

Q2. Attempt any three of the following · 10 × 3 = 30 marks

  1. Describe Three-tier data warehouse architecture with a neat diagram.
  2. Explain Star schema & snowflake schema with suitable example.
  3. Discuss K means algorithm with proper example.
  4. Describe different types of OLAP operations with examples.
  5. Explain web mining in detail

Section C

Q3

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Why data cleaning is required? Discuss the methods of data cleaning.
  2. Describe in detail about the Hardware and Operating Systems for Data Warehousing.

Q4

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Discuss the KDD process in detail.
  2. Assume we have the following dataset of 6 samples, each with features, and a binary class label:
    Sample Age Income Class
    1 25 High No
    2 30 High No
    3 35 Medium Yes
    4 40 Medium Yes
    5 50 Low Yes
    6 60 Low No
    The entropy of the entire data set is 1, Entropy of the splitting attribute
    Age is 1, Weighted Entropy of the splitting attribute Age is 1, Entropy of the splitting attribute Income is 0, Weighted Entropy of the splitting attribute Income is .33. Draw the decision tree.

Q5

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. How distance-based technique is used for data mining?
  2. Transaction List of Items
    T1 I1, I2, I3, I5
    T2 I2, I3, I4
    T3 I4, I5
    T4 I2, I4, I5
    T5 I1, I2, I3, I5
    T6 I1, I2, I3, I4
    Support = 50%, Confidence = 60%
    Generate association rule for given items using Apriori Algorithms.

Q6

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Discuss Hierarchical Clustering in detail.
  2. Write notes on i) STING ii) CLIQUE

Q7

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Discuss in detail the security issues in Data Warehousing.
  2. What is temporal mining? Explain in detail
