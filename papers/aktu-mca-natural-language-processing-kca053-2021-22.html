AKTU MCA Natural Language Processing (KCA053) Question Paper 2021-22

UniversityDr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow
CourseMCA, Semester IV
SubjectNatural Language Processing (KCA053)
ExamTheory Examination 2021-22 (even semester)
Time · Marks3 hours · 100 marks
SourceAKTU question paper, from the ABESIT question paper bank
View original PDF Other years

Note: Attempt all Sections. If you require any missing data, then choose suitably.

Section A

Q1. Attempt all questions in brief · 2 × 10 = 20 marks

  1. List the applications of natural language Processing.
  2. Why the study of language is important?
  3. What do you mean by Knowledge?
  4. Define database system.
  5. What is the role of Grammar in language learning? 3
  6. Define parsing process.
  7. Write about the auxiliary verbs.
  8. What is the advantage of encoding process in language processing.
  9. Define ambiguity process.
  10. What is the importance of logical form in language processing.

Section B

Q2. Attempt any three of the following · 10 × 3 = 30 marks

  1. Explain the Organization of natural language understanding system.
  2. Discuss the process of machine translation with example.
  3. Explain the Augmented transitions network.
  4. Illustrate about the Deterministic Parser in details.
  5. Write in details about the Encoding Ambiguity in logical form.

Section C

Q3

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Write about the Evaluating process of language understanding system.
  2. Explain the different levels of language analysis process.

Q4

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Discuss the process of database interference with example.
  2. Illustrate about the knowledge representation and its techniques.

Q5

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Discuss on the topic of Morphological Analysis in details.
  2. Write down about the Top-down chart parsing with example.

Q6

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Explain the concept of context free Grammar with proper example.
  2. Write short notes on (i) Human preference (ii) verb phrases

Q7

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Discuss about the probabilistic context free grammar.
  2. Explain the concept of Best first parsing process.
