AKTU Pattern Recognition (NCS080) Question Paper 2017-18

UniversityDr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow
CourseB.Tech, Semester VIII
SubjectPattern Recognition (NCS080)
ExamTheory Examination 2017-18 (even semester)
Time · Marks3 hours · 100 marks
SourceAKTU question paper, from the ABESIT question paper bank
View original PDF Other years

Section A

Q1. Attempt all questions in brief · 2 × 10 = 20 marks

  1. Define the law of total probability.
  2. What do you mean by dimension reduction in pattern recognition?
  3. Write the difference between supervised learning and unsupervised learning.
  4. How do we evaluate the performance of a classifier?
  5. What is Hidden Markov Model (HMM)?
  6. What is discriminant function?
  7. Write short notes on Gaussian mixture model.
  8. Discuss cluster validation.
  9. Write K-means clustering algorithm.
  10. Write the difference between clustering and classification.

Section B

Q2. Attempt any three of the following · 10 × 3 = 30 marks

  1. What do you mean by learning and adaptation? Explain the components of a learning system.
  2. Explain the Chi-Square test and discuss their significance in pattern recognition with suitable example.
  3. Explain the concept of expectation maximization with the help of an algorithm.
  4. How K-nearest neighbor (KNN) method works? Explain with KNN estimation and KNN rule.
  5. Explain Naïve Bayes Classifier.

Section C

Q3

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Consider a two-class problem where the classes are labeled pen drive and laptop. Suggest a set of features that could be used to discriminate between these two classes of objects.
  2. What is Baysian Decision Theory? Discuss two-class category Classification in details.

Q4

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Illustrate statistical and syntactic pattern recognition (SPR) approach.
  2. Explain normal density function and discuss its significance in pattern recognition?

Q5

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Write short notes on following- i. Maximum likelihood estimation ii. Bayesian Estimation
  2. What is Parzon window? Explain. Derive the conditions for (i) convergence of mean (ii) convergence of variance.

Q6

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. What do you mean by Fuzzy Decision making? Also discuss the Fuzzy Classification using suitable example.
  2. Name the different methods of non-parameter estimation strategies. What are the main differences between them?

Q7

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. What are different clustering techniques? Why is clustering important? What is an agglomerative clustering algorithm? Explain.
  2. Write short notes on following- i. Cluster validation ii. Criteria function for clustering
