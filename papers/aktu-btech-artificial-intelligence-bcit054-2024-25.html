AKTU Artificial Intelligence (BCIT054) Question Paper 2024-25

UniversityDr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow
CourseB.Tech, Semester V
SubjectArtificial Intelligence (BCIT054)
ExamTheory Examination 2024-25 (odd semester)
Time · Marks3 hours · 70 marks
SourceAKTU question paper, from the ABESIT question paper bank
View original PDF Other years

Note: Attempt all Sections. In case of any missing data; choose suitably.

Section A

Q1. Attempt all questions in brief · 2 × 7 = 14 marks

  1. Define Artificial Intelligence.
  2. What is a deterministic environment in AI?
  3. Define problem-solving in the context of AI.
  4. Define an optimization problem in AI.
  5. What is First Order Predicate Logic (FOPL)?
  6. Define negotiation in the context of software agents.
  7. Explain the concept of machine translation.

Section B

Q2. Attempt any three of the following · 7 × 3 = 21 marks

  1. Discuss the role of sensors and actuators in Intelligent Agent systems with suitable examples.
  2. Explain the difference between uninformed and informed search strategies with examples.
  3. Explain the categories and objects in ontological engineering with examples.
  4. Describe the negotiation process in multi-agent systems with examples.
  5. Explain the architecture and functioning of speech recognition systems.

Section C

Q3

Attempt any one part of the following · 7 × 1 = 7 marks

  1. Explain the role of AI in tackling global challenges like climate change and resource management.
  2. Explain the role of AI in enhancing decision-making processes in business applications.

Q4

Attempt any one part of the following · 7 × 1 = 7 marks

  1. Discuss the structure and components of a Constraint Satisfaction Problem (CSP).
  2. Discuss the alpha-beta pruning technique and how it improves game search efficiency.

Q5

Attempt any one part of the following · 7 × 1 = 7 marks

  1. Compare semantic networks and frames as knowledge representation methods. Explain how FOPL is used for representing knowledge in AI systems.
  2. Explain how FOPL is used for representing knowledge in AI systems.

Q6

Attempt any one part of the following · 7 × 1 = 7 marks

  1. Explain how argumentation is used for resolving conflicts in multi-agent systems.
  2. Discuss the use of trust metrics in multi-agent decision-making.

Q7

Attempt any one part of the following · 7 × 1 = 7 marks

  1. Discuss the challenges of multilingual support in machine translation systems.
  2. Explain how tokenization impacts natural language processing tasks.
