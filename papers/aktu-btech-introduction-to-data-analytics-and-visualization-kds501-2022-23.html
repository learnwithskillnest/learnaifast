AKTU Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization (KDS501) Question Paper 2022-23

UniversityDr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow
CourseB.Tech, Semester V
SubjectIntroduction to Data Analytics and Visualization (KDS501)
ExamTheory Examination 2022-23 (odd semester)
Time · Marks3 hours · 100 marks
SourceAKTU question paper, from the ABESIT question paper bank
View original PDF Other years

Note: Attempt all Sections. If you require any missing data, then choose suitably.

Section A

Q1. Attempt all questions in brief · 2 × 10 = 20 marks

  1. List the features of big data analytics platform.
  2. Why there is need of data analytics?
  3. What are the components of time series?
  4. List various types of regression analysis techniques.
  5. What is sentiment analysis?
  6. What are problem in Flajolet-Martin (FM) algorithm?
  7. What is Market Basket Analysis?
  8. Explain hierarchical method of clustering.
  9. Define sharding and database shard.
  10. List any two visualization tools.

Section B

Q2. Attempt any three of the following · 10 × 3 = 30 marks

  1. Differentiate between structured, semi-structured and unstructured data.
  2. Describe grid-based fuzzy model.
  3. Explain Alon-Matias-Szegedy Algorithm for second moments on the given stream {a, b, c, b, d, a, c, d, a, b, d, c, a, a, b}.
  4. Explain market basket analysis with an example.
  5. Discuss various classification of visualization techniques.

Section C

Q3

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Discuss all the moderntools of data analytic.
  2. Describe all the phases of data analytics life cycle.

Q4

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Describe PCA algorithm. Compute the principal component using PCA algorithm on given data as:
    CLASS 1: X = 2 , 3 , 4
    Y = 1 , 5 , 3
    CLASS 2:
    X = 5 , 6 , 7
    Y = 6 , 7 , 8
  2. Illustrate Bayesian network with an example.

Q5

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Describe briefly the architecture of Data Stream Management System (DSMS).
  2. Explain any one algorithm for counting oneness in a window.

Q6

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. For the given data, find the association rule using apriori algorithm.
  2. What do you mean by k-means clustering? How does the k-means algorithm work? Write k-meansalgorithm for partitioning.

Q7

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Define HDFS. Discuss the HDFS architecture and HDFS commands in brief.
  2. Differentiate between Map Reduce and Apache Pig.
