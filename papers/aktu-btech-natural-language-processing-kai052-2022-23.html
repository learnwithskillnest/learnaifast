AKTU Natural Language Processing (KAI052) Question Paper 2022-23

UniversityDr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow
CourseB.Tech, Semester V
SubjectNatural Language Processing (KAI052)
ExamTheory Examination 2022-23 (odd semester)
Time · Marks3 hours · 100 marks
SourceAKTU question paper, from the ABESIT question paper bank
View original PDF Other years

Note: Attempt all Sections. If you require any missing data, then choose suitably.

Section A

Q1. Attempt all questions in brief · 2 × 10 = 20 marks

  1. Differentiate between bigram and trigram.
  2. Define the N-grams model.
  3. What are the problems with PCFG?
  4. What do you understand by ambiguity?
  5. Give a detailed account of similarities and differences among the following set of lexemes: imitation, synthetic, artificial, fake and simulated.
  6. Explain Word Sense Disambiguation.
  7. Explain Articulatory Phonetics.
  8. What are the reasons to use short-time fourier transform.
  9. List the various issues of machine translation system.
  10. What is discourse planning?

Section B

Q2. Attempt any three of the following · 10 × 3 = 30 marks

  1. Explain the need of smoothing and if we are given the following corpus: <s> I am Sam </s> <s> Sam I am </s> <s> I do not like green eggs and Sam </s> Using a bigram language model with Laplace smoothing, what is P(Sam |am)? Include <s> and </s> in your counts just like any other token.
  2. Discuss the relative advantages and disadvantages of deep versus shallow parsing. Also list various types of parsers. Explain in detail.
  3. Discuss various relations among the word senses. Also discuss various knowledge sources in WSD.
  4. Explain speech coding and speech synthesis with pattern matching in detail.
  5. Explain different pattern comparison techniques in detail. Also elaborate on Cepstral distances, and Weighted Cepstral distances.

Section C

Q3

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Write an algorithm for parsing a finite-state transducer using the pseudo code. Explain the algorithm with an example.
  2. Write an algorithm for simple top-down parser with an example.

Q4

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Given the grammar and lexicon below, show the final chart for the followingsentence after applying the bottom-up chart parser.Remember that the finalchart contains all edges added during theparsing process. You may useeither the notation from class (i.e.nodes/links) or the notation from the book todepict the chart. S → VP VP → Verb NP NP → NP PP NP → Det Noun PP → Prep Noun Det → the Verb → Find Prep → in Noun → men | suits Find the men in suits.
  2. Give an account of CYK parser

Q5

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Discuss various knowledge sources in WSD.
  2. Between the words eat and find which would you expect to be more effectivein selecting restriction-based sense disambiguation.

Q6

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Define Articulatory Phonetics. Also explain production and classification of speech sounds in detail. Give examples.
  2. Write regular expressions for the following languages. (i) the set of all alphabetic strings; (ii) the set of all lower case alphabetic strings ending in a b; (iii)the set of all strings from the alphabet a,b such that each a is immediately preceded by and immediately followed by a b; Explain how these regular expressions will be used in speech processing.

Q7

Attempt any one part of the following · 10 × 1 = 10 marks

  1. Explain different feature extraction and pattern comparison techniques used in speech analysis. Also elaborate on likelihood distortions, and spectral distortion, in detail.
  2. Explain hidden markovmodel with Baum-Welch parameter re- estimation. Also elaborate on its implementation issues.
