    How it works

    • Finds your sections: summary, experience, projects, education, skills, certifications and achievements.
    • Keeps the strongest bullets: points with numbers and results ("cut load time by 40%") are kept first, and long bullets are shortened.
    • Drops filler: hobbies, declarations, references and personal details that recruiters skip.
    • Fits one page: adjusts font size and trims older entries until everything fits on one A4 page.

    Check the result before you send it. The tool keeps your own words but cannot judge which project matters most for a specific job, so edit freely.
