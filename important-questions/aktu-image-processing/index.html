Are these AKTU Image Processing questions guaranteed to come in the exam?
No. They are the questions that repeated most in past papers, which makes them a strong hint, but AKTU can ask anything from the syllabus. Use this list to prioritise, not to skip units.

How was this important questions list made?
We compared all 136 questions from 6 AKTU Image Processing papers (2016-17 to 2025-26) and grouped questions that ask about the same topic, then counted how many papers each appeared in.
