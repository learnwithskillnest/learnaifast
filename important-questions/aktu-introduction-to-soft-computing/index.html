The questions AKTU asks again and again in Introduction to Soft Computing, found by comparing every question in 6 previous year papers from 2016-17 to 2022-23 (subject codes EOE041, KOE036, KOE046, NOE041).

How this list is made: we compared all 142 questions in these papers and grouped the ones that ask about the same thing. Each item shows how many papers it appeared in, which years, and other ways it was worded. Topics that come back often are a strong hint, not a guarantee, so study your full syllabus too.
