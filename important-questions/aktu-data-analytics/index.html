AKTU Data Analytics Important Questions

The questions AKTU asks again and again in Data Analytics, found by comparing every question in 10 previous year papers from 2020-21 to 2025-26 (subject codes BADS601, BCS052, BIT601, KCS051, KIT601).

How this list is made: we compared all 235 questions in these papers and grouped the ones that ask about the same thing. Each item shows how many papers it appeared in, which years, and other ways it was worded. Topics that come back often are a strong hint, not a guarantee, so study your full syllabus too.

Frequently asked questions

Are these AKTU Data Analytics questions guaranteed to come in the exam?
No. They are the questions that repeated most in past papers, which makes them a strong hint, but AKTU can ask anything from the syllabus. Use this list to prioritise, not to skip units.

How was this important questions list made?
We compared all 235 questions from 10 AKTU Data Analytics papers (2020-21 to 2025-26) and grouped questions that ask about the same topic, then counted how many papers each appeared in.
