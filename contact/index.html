Before you write

A tool isn't working on my phone.
Try the latest version of Chrome or Safari. iPhone photos in HEIC format may not open; share or save them as JPG first. If it still fails, email us with the subject "Tool problem".

Are your exam photo sizes official?
They're taken from each exam's latest notices and guides, and dated on the resizer page. Always compare with your exam's official notification before uploading.

Can you send me the answers to a question paper?
We don't publish full solutions, but our AKTU exam prep guide shows how to use free AI tools to check your own answers.
